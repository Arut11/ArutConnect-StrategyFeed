# Strategy sources and adaptation notes

The initial online profiles are ARUT adaptations built from the locally pinned Flowseal 1.10.2 strategy structure. They combine broad Discord Voice UDP coverage with technique families currently used in public zapret community configurations (including hostfakesplit/fooling=ts and fakedsplit/fooling=ts).

They are intentionally stored as declarative definitions rather than third-party BAT or executable files.
